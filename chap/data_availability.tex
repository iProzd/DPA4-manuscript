\section{Data availability}

The MPtrj, WBM, OMat24, OMol25 and SPICE-MACE-OFF datasets are publicly available from the original sources cited in the Methods.
The trained DPA4 checkpoints used for the reported Matbench Discovery, OMat24, OMol25 and SPICE-MACE-OFF results are available at AI Square (\url{https://www.aissquare.com/models/detail?pageType=models&name=DPA4-Paper&id=436}).

\section{Code availability}

The DPA4 implementation used for training and inference is available in the DeePMD-kit repository (\url{https://github.com/deepmodeling/deepmd-kit}) from version 3.2.0 onward.
