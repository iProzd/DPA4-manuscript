\begin{abstract}
    Machine-learning interatomic potentials now approach quantum-mechanical accuracy on standard benchmarks, but the training cost of the most expressive equivariant architectures has become a serious bottleneck.
    We present DPA4, an SO(2)-equivariant interatomic-potential architecture combining a low-rank edge--node SO(2)-equivariant product, a multi-focus design for message non-linearity, envelope-gated attention for message aggregation and a Lebedev-grid SO(3)-equivariant non-linearity.
    A compiler-friendly conservative-force training path gives up to $\sim$3 times wall-clock speedup under \texttt{torch.compile}.
    On the compliant Matbench Discovery benchmark, DPA4-Pro attains the best Combined Performance Score (CPS) on the leaderboard, while the 2.76M-parameter DPA4-Air exceeds the accuracy of the 30.1M-parameter eSEN-30M-MP baseline with 10.9$\times$ fewer parameters and 42.9$\times$ less training compute.
    On SPICE-MACE-OFF, DPA4-Plus gives the lowest aggregate molecular energy and force errors among the compared models.
    Together these results place DPA4 on a new accuracy--cost Pareto frontier on Matbench Discovery and position it as a strong candidate backbone for future multi-task large atomistic model (LAM) pretraining.
\end{abstract}
