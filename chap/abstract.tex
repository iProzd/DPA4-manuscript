\begin{abstract}
    Machine-learning interatomic potentials now approach quantum-mechanical accuracy on standard benchmarks, but the training cost of the most expressive equivariant architectures has become a serious bottleneck.
    We introduce DPA4, an SE(3)-equivariant interatomic-potential architecture with an EMFA (Edge-conditioned, Multi-Focus, Attention) SO(2)-equivariant convolution that combines a low-rank edge--node SO(2)-equivariant product, a multi-focus design for message nonlinearity, and envelope-gated attention for message aggregation.
    A Wigner bilinear network supplies the SO(3)-equivariant nonlinearity and, evaluated on an exact Lebedev quadrature grid, preserves equivariance to machine precision.
    A compiler-friendly conservative energy-gradient training path provides up to $\sim$3 times wall-clock speedup under \texttt{torch.compile}.
    On the compliant Matbench Discovery benchmark, DPA4-Pro leads every ranked leaderboard metric, including the Combined Performance Score (CPS), while the 8.8M-parameter DPA4-Plus matches the strongest baseline, EquiformerV3+DeNS-MP, with 3.4$\times$ fewer parameters and 5.1$\times$ less training compute, and the 5.1M-parameter DPA4-Air exceeds the accuracy of the 30.1M-parameter eSEN-30M-MP baseline with 20.9$\times$ less training compute.
    On SPICE-MACE-OFF, the 8.8M-parameter DPA4-Plus lowers the aggregate molecular energy and force errors of the 6.5M-parameter eSEN baseline by 32\% each, and the 1.1M-parameter DPA4-Neo already matches the 3.2M-parameter eSEN baseline with 2.8$\times$ fewer parameters.
    Together these results place DPA4 on a new accuracy--cost Pareto frontier on Matbench Discovery and position it as a strong candidate backbone for future multi-task large atomistic model (LAM) pretraining.
\end{abstract}
